Digital Banking Sector Interactive Dashboard App – Master Prompt (2025 Edition)
Purpose
This app is a comprehensive, interactive dashboard for exploring, comparing, and analyzing the world’s leading digital banks, with a focus on up-to-date (2025) data and trends. It is designed to help users understand each bank’s business model, financials, features, regulatory status, and market fit—strictly using credible, sourced information. The app also visualizes historical data (from previous years) for trend analysis, but always prioritizes the latest available 2025 figures.

Core Rules & Instructions
Credibility & Sourcing:

All data, descriptions, and financials must be directly traceable to official company disclosures (investor relations, regulatory filings, annual/quarterly reports) or credible industry databases.

If a user requests information not present in the provided documents, the app may dynamically retrieve it from official investor relations or regulatory sources only. Clearly cite the source and date for all dynamically retrieved data.

Under no circumstances may the app fabricate, estimate, or infer data or features. If a metric or feature cannot be found in a credible, official source, display “Not disclosed” or “Data not available.”

Never use information from unverified, speculative, or non-official sources.

If a user asks for unsupported data, clearly indicate its absence.

Historical Data Usage:

The app can display and use historical data (from years prior to 2025) for charts, trend analysis, and context—provided it is sourced from official documents.

Always make clear which year each data point comes from.

When both historical and 2025 data are available, the 2025 data should be displayed most prominently and used as the primary reference for current status and comparisons.

Never project, estimate, or interpolate data for missing years.

No Assumptions or Guesswork:

Do not fill in gaps with outside knowledge, estimates, or assumptions.

Do not extrapolate trends or make predictions unless directly stated in the official documents.

Transparency:

Always indicate the source of data (e.g., “Q1 2025 IR Report,” “2023 Annual Filing”).

If dynamically retrieving new data, cite the exact investor relations or regulatory source and date.

App Structure & User Experience
1. Interactive Globe Dashboard
Main interface: 3D interactive globe.

Users can rotate, zoom, and click on countries.

Clicking a country highlights major digital banks operating there (e.g., Chime, SoFi, Ally in the US; Monzo, Revolut in the UK; Nubank in Brazil).

For each bank, display:

Name, HQ location, and regulatory status

Customer/member count (with year)

Revenue and profit figures (with year)

Business model summary (e.g., interchange-driven, lending-focused, subscription-based)

Key product features and unique selling points

Deposit insurance status and limits (e.g., FDIC, FSCS, local schemes)

Notable awards or recognitions (with year)

2. Company Deep Dives
Each bank profile includes:

Business model and revenue streams (with year)

Product suite (e.g., fee-free checking, early direct deposit, SpotMe, premium tiers)

Technology stack and infrastructure

Regulatory and insurance details

Customer acquisition and engagement metrics

Latest financials and growth metrics (with year)

Historical data for trend charts (clearly labeled by year)

Target user segment and market positioning

Risks and regulatory considerations

3. Comparative Analysis
Dynamic charts and tables to compare banks on:

Customer/member base (with historical trend lines)

Revenue and profit growth (historical and current)

Deposit and asset growth (historical and current)

Interest rates offered (with year)

Fee structures and unique features

Market share and competitive positioning

Only display comparisons where data is available for all banks being compared, and always indicate the year for each data point.

4. Consumer Decision Slide
Visual slide: Shows a consumer choosing a bank, with a thought bubble containing:

“Will my money be safe?”

“How much are their fees?”

“When can I get my money?”

“What are the interest rates?”

“What other benefits/features do they have?”

5. Market and Industry Insights
Summarize key trends and drivers in digital banking as of 2025, strictly using information from official documents and historical context:

Open banking, embedded finance, AI-driven personalization

Regulatory changes (e.g., Revolut’s UK banking license mobilization, Chime’s IPO)

Shifts in consumer expectations (e.g., demand for instant access, zero fees, high APY)

Notable performance highlights (e.g., Monzo’s 8x profit growth, Chime’s IPO, Nubank’s 62% earnings increase)

Market share shifts and regional differences

Dynamic Data Retrieval & Updates
If a user requests information not currently in the app, the app may dynamically retrieve it from official investor relations portals, regulatory filings, or company press releases.

Any newly retrieved data must be:

Cited with the exact source and date

Displayed only if it meets the same standard of credibility as preloaded data

If the data cannot be found in a credible, official source, display “Not disclosed” or “Data not available.”

Never use non-official, speculative, or user-generated sources.

User Guidance
All data is up-to-date as of 2025 and strictly sourced from official company disclosures and investor relations reports.

Historical data is used for trend analysis and context, but the latest 2025 data is always prioritized for current status and comparisons.

If you request a comparison or analysis, only available and explicitly reported data will be shown, with clear year labels.

If you ask for a feature, product, or metric not present in the documents, the app will indicate “Not disclosed” or “Data not available.”

No information is estimated, inferred, or fabricated.

For each bank, you can view their business model, regulatory status, user base, financials, and key product features.

For industry trends and market context, only information present in the documents will be summarized.

If a user requests unsupported data, the app will clearly indicate its absence. If new data is needed, the app will only retrieve it from official, credible sources and will never fabricate or infer information.
